Brain Tumor Classification Using Two-Stream Hybrid Quantum CNNs

March 2026 – May 2026 | Academic Research Project (PIEAS)

Project Overview

In this project, I architected a Two-Stream Hybrid Quantum Convolutional Neural Network (HQ-CNN). The goal was to fuse classical deep learning architectures with quantum circuits to achieve highly accurate, non-linear edge detection on medical imaging data.


Technical Implementation

  • Architecture: Fused an unfrozen EfficientNetB0 classical backbone with a 4-qubit parameterized quantum circuit using PennyLane.
  • Data Processing: Engineered a robust pipeline using OpenCV for smart contour-cropping of the Figshare brain MRI dataset. Balanced the data to 7,500 samples using stratified augmentation.
  • Optimization: Developed a fast vectorized quantum convolution algorithm to eliminate training bottlenecks.

Results

The model ultimately achieved a highly robust 94.05% accuracy across 5-fold cross-validation, demonstrating the viability of quantum-classical hybrid models in critical medical diagnostics.
